# The app idea

## Why make it?

Many people enjoy fitness but do not have friends who enjoy the same activities. Someone might have a gym friend but still want a running or hiking friend. Finding people who share more than one interest can be hard.

Fitness Friend Finder would help people meet others nearby who want to do similar activities **as friends**. It is for adults and is meant for friendship, not dating.

## A simple journey

1. A person signs up and fills out a profile. They add their age, photos, sports, and a little about their life. They can also share things such as music taste, other hobbies, or what they study.
2. They describe each activity separately. For example, running might be something they love and do often, while climbing might be something they tried once and want to do more.
3. The app asks to use the phone's location when they open it. If they do not want that, they can enter a city instead. They set how far away they are willing to look.
4. They can set preferences for the people they want to see, such as sports, gender, and age.
5. The app suggests people with shared interests who are close enough for both people. A person can like or pass on each suggestion.
6. If two people like each other, they can chat and decide whether to meet for an activity.

The first version would focus on helping people find and talk to potential friends. Miami is the first place to focus on getting people to join, but the app does not have to be limited to Miami. Posts, health data, and calendars come later.

## What would make it useful?

The suggestions should help someone find a person they could actually do something with. Shared sports and a practical distance should matter most. Music, hobbies, college, and similar details could help two people connect, but should count less than sports.
