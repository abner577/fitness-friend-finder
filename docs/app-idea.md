# The app idea

## Why make it?

Many people enjoy fitness but do not have friends who enjoy the same activities. Someone might have a gym friend but still want a running or hiking friend. Finding people who share more than one interest can be hard.

Fitness Friend Finder would help people meet others nearby who want to do similar activities **as friends**. It is for adults and is meant for friendship, not dating.

## A simple journey

1. A person signs up and fills out a profile. They add their age, a little about themselves, their hobbies, and the sports they enjoy.
2. They mark which sports matter most to them. For example, running might be their main activity, while they go to the gym occasionally.
3. They choose an area or let the phone use their current location. They set how far away they are willing to look.
4. They can set preferences for the people they want to see, such as sports, gender, and age.
5. The app suggests people with shared interests who are close enough for both people. A person can like or pass on each suggestion.
6. If two people like each other, they can chat and decide whether to meet for an activity.

The first version would focus on helping people find and talk to potential friends. Posting workouts and bringing in activity data from other apps are ideas for later.

## What would make it useful?

The suggestions should help someone find a person they could actually do something with. Shared sports, how much each person enjoys them, and a practical distance matter more than showing a large number of profiles.
